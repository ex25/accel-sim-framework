description: Record an Accel-Sim research change so a later Codex session can recover its rationale, evidence, and limitations.
---

Update the appropriate source of truth: compact facts in `knowledge/`, full
explanation in `docs/`, reusable procedure in `skills/`, design rationale in
`DECISIONS.md`, and phase history in `CHANGELOG.md`. Include date, branch,
commit (or pending), commands, tests, evidence labels, and known limitations.
Do not paste large logs, traces, binaries, or duplicated manuals.
